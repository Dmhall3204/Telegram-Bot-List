A mixture of experts, directing a mixture of mixtures of experts.

A Qwen3-class sparse MoE Conductor with 128 internal experts sends every query to a pool of five specialists: four MoE models and one dense fast path. Each MoE specialist then runs its own token-level gating across its own experts. The Conductor combines, weights and synthesises what they return.

How a request moves

The Conductor reads every query and emits a weighted distribution over specialist clusters. The selected specialists fire in parallel, and each one routes tokens through its own internal expert gate. A fast path fires alongside on every request and sets a latency floor. When more than one specialist answers, the Conductor runs again as the synthesiser and merges their outputs, weighted by its original routing confidence.

§ 01 · Architecture

Two orchestration tiers of gating, plus one inherited.

The framework routes coarsely across four specialist clusters at L0, and an always-on fast path runs beside them. Every selected MoE model then does its own learned token-level gating at L2. That tier comes free with choosing sparse-activation specialists. This is hierarchical MoE lifted from the layer level to the system level.

§ 02 · L0 · Conductor

The top-level gate.

The Conductor is the only model that reads every query. It is a sparse MoE itself, with 128 experts and top-8 routing, so even the routing decision comes from an internal expert gate rather than a dense forward pass. About 3.3B parameters activate per token, which keeps a call on every request cheap. It always emits a full distribution over clusters, never a hard pick.

Tier L0

Conductor · Qwen3-30B-A3B class

The Conductor reads the query, classifies intent, splits it into per-cluster subqueries, and emits a probability distribution across the clusters. When more than one cluster fires, the same model runs again as the synthesiser. Each specialist output reaches it annotated with that cluster's probability, so the merge is weighted rather than a flat concatenation. That gives two roles from one model and one warm pool.

§ 03 · L1 · Specialist models

Five specialists, all open-weight.

Every specialist except the fast path is a Mixture-of-Experts. The current rotation comes from the Qwen3 family and changes as better open-weight options ship. The stable part is the shape of the pool: each role, its architecture class, and the behaviour the role must guarantee. Specific checkpoints are an implementation detail.

01 · Code · agentic

Code Specialist

Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B-Instruct · 30.5B / 3.3B active · 128 experts · 256K native (1M via YaRN)

Agentic code and repo-scale reasoning, with native function calling and tool use. Tuned for instruction-following without a thinking pass. Code screenshots are read by the Vision Specialist, and the two outputs merge at synthesis.

02 · Reasoning · thinking mode

Reasoning Specialist

Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Thinking · 80B / 3B active · 512 + 1 shared experts · 262K ctx

A hybrid MoE with Gated DeltaNet and Gated Attention layers in a 3:1 ratio, built for chain-of-thought. Only about 3B of its 80B parameters activate per token, so a request costs less than the size suggests. It thinks before it answers, and that trace stays internal unless the user asks to see it.

03 · Vision · multimodal

Vision Specialist

Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B-Instruct · 31B / 3B active · VL-MoE · served in BF16

Handles images, OCR, charts, screenshots and visual Q&A natively, with no external OCR pipeline in front of it. The vision tower runs at full precision. Any request with an image attached always includes this specialist.

04 · Long context · RAG

Long-Context Specialist

Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 · 30.5B / 3.3B active · 256K native (1M via YaRN)

Handles RAG payloads, full codebases, long transcripts and multi-document summaries. It shares its weight topology with the Conductor but does a different job: retrieval and long-document synthesis, not classification. Context past the threshold always includes it.

Fast · shadow

Latency Floor

Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 · 4B dense · no internal gating · sub-200 ms TTFT target

Dense on purpose: dense models reach the first token sooner. It fires on every request alongside L0. Trivial queries get its answer directly. For complex queries its answer goes into the synthesis prompt as context, and it is never used as a blind fallback.

Why the lineup works

The four MoE specialists each activate about 3B parameters per token. Even with two firing in parallel, the compute load is close to running a small model. The 80B reasoning specialist has the most capacity, but at 3B active it doesn't dominate latency or cost. The dense fast path stays under 5B and has no gating overhead.

§ 04 · Cluster organisation

Four routable clusters, one always on.

The router can send work to four clusters: code, reasoning, vision and long-ctx. The fifth, fast, is never routed to. It fires on every request, outside the capacity budget. That keeps cost accounting clean: capacity is bounded on the routable side, and the shadow path is a constant.

§ 05 · The sophistication layer

Six MoE techniques, applied to orchestration.

Each technique below comes from the MoE training and serving literature. Each one maps onto a concrete behaviour of the system, implemented in the reference service.

Soft routing with weights

L0 emits a full softmax over the routable clusters. Clusters at p ≥ 0.10 are candidates, and the synthesiser uses their probabilities as merge priors.

↳ top-k gating · router.plan

Capacity factor

At most 2 concurrent L1 specialists per request, separate from the always-on shadow. If three clusters clear the threshold, only the top two fire. An open circuit breaker passes its slot to the next-ranked cluster.

↳ expert capacity limit · capacity_factor: 2

Load-balance surveillance

Every specialist invocation is counted. If a cluster's share of routable traffic falls below 5% or rises above 60% over a rolling 24 hours, an alert fires and the router prompt gets reviewed.

↳ auxiliary load-balancing loss · Monitor

Shadow path as latency floor

The Latency Floor fires on every request, outside the capacity budget, and streams into a buffer. Trivial queries replay it live. Complex ones attach it to synthesis. A specialist that fails before its first token falls back to it.

↳ shared expert with guaranteed activation · FastShadow

Probability-weighted synthesis

When k > 1 specialists answer, the synthesis prompt lists each output in weight order, annotated with its probability. The synthesiser is primed to trust higher-weighted outputs more. This happens in the forward pass only, with no gradients.

↳ gated expert combination · _synthesis_messages

Routing-collapse detection

A real-time monitor flags pathological distributions: one cluster above 80% or any below 2% over the last hour. It then switches every replica to uniform-mix routing for 15 minutes and requests a manual prompt review.

↳ dead-expert detection · uniform_hold_s

§ 06 · Commitments

Private. Open-weight. User-funded.

db2productions is funded directly by its users. Subscription revenue pays for the three things that make the product better: stronger models, lower latency, and a cleaner product. There are no ads, no data sales, and no upstream vendor deciding what you can or can't ask.

Better models

We swap in newer, stronger open-weight specialists as soon as they prove out on our evaluations. The framework is model-agnostic by design. The roles stay fixed while the checkpoints behind them change.

Lower latency

Warmer pools at peak hours, faster hardware as it becomes viable, better caching and streaming. Every fraction of a second saved is a measurable win for the person waiting on a response.

Better experience

Cleaner chat, richer history, better exports, a more responsive mobile app, better prompt tooling. The interface is where you spend your time, so it gets real investment.

Private · self-hosted

Every specialist runs on inference infrastructure db2productions operates directly. Nothing is routed through consumer AI APIs. Conversations are not logged for training. When you delete your history, it is purged from active storage immediately and from backups within 30 days.

Open · auditable

Every specialist in the pool is an open-weight model under the Apache-2.0 licence. There are no proprietary black boxes, no vendor lock-in and no sudden API deprecations. The framework layer is proprietary, and the model layer can be audited by anyone.
